# Personal brand plan: IELTS teacher on Instagram

**Goal:** people in Uzbekistan recognise your face as *the* IELTS teacher to learn from, and a steady flow of them books lessons, online or in your city.

**The core idea:** every reel shows **you** teaching **one** useful thing in under 40 seconds, and ends with **one** low-effort next step (comment a keyword, or join the free mock test). Animations are seasoning, not the meal.

---

## 1. The funnel

```
Reel (you, one tip)  →  Follow / save  →  Story or DM keyword  →  Telegram  →  Free mock test / trial lesson  →  Paid course
```

- **Free entry offer** (choose one and keep it the same for 30 days): a free 15-minute Speaking mock with a band estimate, or a free "Speaking Part 2: 50 topics + model answers" PDF sent in Telegram.
- **Keyword CTA:** "Izohga **MOCK** deb yozing, Telegramdan yozaman" (write MOCK in the comments). Comments also push reach.
- **Paid offers:** online group, offline group in your city, 1:1. Put the schedule and next start date in a pinned post and a highlight.
- **Parents matter:** 45.6% of the first reel's viewers were 13–17. Make some content that a parent would forward: results, how a course works, your credentials.

## 5. Recording guide (your side)

- **Phone:** vertical, back camera, 1080×1920 at 30 fps (4K is fine), lens wiped.
- **Light:** face the window; no light behind you.
- **Sound** matters most: a clip-on lavalier mic is the best small investment. Record in a quiet room with soft furniture.
- **Framing:** eyes about a third from the top, head and shoulders, leave space at the top for the hook text and at the bottom for captions.
- **Batching:** record 6–8 videos in one session per week. Change your top or the background between takes so they don't look identical.
- **Delivery:** say the hook first, with energy. Don't introduce yourself at the start ("Assalomu alaykum, men…" loses viewers). Pause half a second between sentences; I cut the gaps.
- **Send me:** the raw file plus the script or bullet points you said. Your script makes Uzbek captions exact; automatic Uzbek transcription is unreliable.

## 6. My side: the editing pipeline

Built on the same Remotion setup as the Ali reel, with a reusable teacher-reel template:
- Silence and stumble cuts, punch-in zooms on key words, word-by-word captions in the brand style
- Hook text on frame 0, animated example cards (wrong ✗ / right ✓, band comparisons, vocabulary)
- Occasional Ali cameo (for example, Ali makes the mistake and you correct him), light SFX and music bed
- Branded end card with the CTA, plus a cover image for the grid
- Output: MP4 ready for Reels, plus a cover PNG

## 8. What to measure (every Sunday, 10 minutes)

| Metric | Why | Episode 1 baseline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Still watching at 3 s and 7 s | Is the hook working? | about 50% at 7 s |
| Average watch time | Is the middle holding? | 13 s |
| Follows per 1,000 views | Does the profile convince people? | 0 |
| Saves + shares | Is it useful enough to keep or forward? | 3 saves, 0 shares |
| Keyword comments / DMs → Telegram joins → trial lessons → enrolments | Does it turn into income? | — |

Keep what beats the average and do more of it; drop formats that lose half the audience before 3 s.
